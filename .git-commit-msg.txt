Compare Workspace titles and refuse hubs that copy anchor titles (#96)

S03 of #93.

I6 leaves the single tree: a checked Idea title is compared with every title
of its own Workspace, through a read-only title context. Candidates come from
an inverted bigram index and the existing Dice function still decides (0.8, or
an exact normalized equality); an unordered pair is reported once, on the later
checked Card; only pairs touching the checked Cards are reported, and a
context Card is never the subject of a line. --cards builds the context from
its one complete export_titles read and refuses a partial or changing
catalogue; --json accepts the context as a {"cards": ..., "context": ...}
bundle; the entry contract gains workspace. digest-audit passes the complete
title projection it already read, so its I6 is Workspace-wide with no second
read.

Hub Cards gain the anchor-title fault: a line or topic hub whose title repeats
the title of the Card it hangs under, with whitespace collapsed and case
ignored and never broad similarity. get_hub reports it read-only, and the hub
compiler's precheck refuses a new draft that has it, naming the anchor.

Tests: two zt-check unit tests (context scope, one line per pair), a public
zt-check --cards test (workspace-titles.tests.ps1), a hub_cards.rs test of the
read-only report, and three precheck cases in hub-compiler.tests.ps1.
